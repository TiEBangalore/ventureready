Positioning statement
For tier-2 city diagnostic chains (20–80 beds) that can't justify per-device monitoring hardware, Ferra delivers continuous remote vitals at a per-bed monthly price — unlike Philips or GE rigs that need heavy capex and a biomedical engineer on staff.
ICP trigger event

Incumbents sell hardware per device on long procurement cycles. We're software-led on low-cost sensors, billed per occupied bed — the chain pays only for what it uses, scales month to month, and needs no biomedical engineer.
GTM first 10 customers
Warm intros from our clinical advisor into the three named chains, opening with a paid 30-bed pilot per chain. Per-bed price ~₹900/bed/month; pilot ACV ~₹1.5L, expanding branch by branch as each chain adds ICU capacity.
